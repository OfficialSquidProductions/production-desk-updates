Production Desk 1.5.12

  • Lined Script gains configurable keyboard shortcuts in Settings, stable undo/redo scrolling, natural scene/shot numbering, clear line-to-label connectors, gutter positioning and adjustable label opacity.
  • New coverage lines create distinct shots. Search and autocomplete existing shot links, renumber shots with collision handling, and keep deleted shots out of link choices.
  • Navigate the Shot List with Up/Down arrows while retaining your place. Clicking shots preserves scroll, and editing controls keep their own arrow-key behavior.
  • Shooting Schedule strips have larger grab areas and drop targets, visible placement feedback, edge autoscroll and safe cancellation. Scene editing and color controls remain available.
  • Import beside Save As restores complete Production Desk projects, original scripts/PDFs, shots, schedules, elements/cast, annotations, labels, links and project settings. Internal IDs survive duplicate imports. Pending edits save first; failed or cancelled imports preserve current work.
  • The original-PDF Main Breakdown fixes from 1.5.11 remain included. Existing projects, undo/redo, portable backups and offline operation are preserved. App Store and TestFlight submissions are unchanged.
