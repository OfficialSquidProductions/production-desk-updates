Production Desk 1.5.14

Arrange shots across scenes in the new Shot Shooting Order tab. Broad strip targets, large grab handles, clear insertion feedback, edge scrolling and an exact-placement dialog make filming order easy to adjust. Escape cancels a drag; keyboard navigation and Undo/Redo retain your place.

Shot List remains the canonical list. Both tabs edit the same shot details, while filming order is saved separately using shot IDs. Reordering keeps shot identifiers, lined-script links and scene shooting schedules intact. New shots append to filming order, and deleted shots are removed.

Scene, day, status and search filters remain available. Filtered moves retain the relative order of hidden shots. Complete .productiondesk project files and full backups carry the arrangement, shot records, original PDFs and linked coverage between computers.

All Production Desk 1.5.13 fixes remain included: Shot List deletion and undo/redo context, paginated Main Breakdown with optional Continuous Scroll, separated Settings controls and readable lined-script gutter exports. Existing projects, backups and offline operation are preserved. App Store and TestFlight submissions are unchanged.
