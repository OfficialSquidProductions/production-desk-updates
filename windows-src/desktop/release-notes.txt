Production Desk 1.5.0

Lined scripts are now part of the regular Mac and Windows app:
- Keep original PDF pages, link straight or wavy coverage lines to shots, and move labels into margins.
- Draw, highlight, add arrows and notes, and share camera movements with the shot list.
- Export lined PDFs with original text and page geometry preserved.
- Preserve original PDFs and editable annotations in full JSON backups.
- Keep independent annotations for different PDF revisions.

Existing productions and schedules are preserved. PDF revisions require manual mark transfer.
