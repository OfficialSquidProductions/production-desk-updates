Production Desk 1.5.18 (49) for Mac

Scripts now load into your current production instead of creating an imported copy. Repeated imports preserve matching scene IDs, breakdown edits, shots, schedule positions and original PDF annotations.

Lined Script annotation boxes show Shot Size by default. Settings now includes checkboxes for camera movement and shot descriptions; enabled fields also appear in exported lined PDFs, including long descriptions.

Renumbering a shot resets the next automatic number. Change 4.3 to 7.1 to continue with 7.2 and 7.3; change again to 3.2 to continue with 3.3 and 3.4. The baseline is saved with the production.

This update is for the direct-download Mac version only. Windows and App Store releases are held for further testing.
