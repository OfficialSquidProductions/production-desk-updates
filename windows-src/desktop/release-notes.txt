Production Desk 1.5.1

- Report bugs and crashes from Production settings with a required description and a reviewable email draft to alexanderhosier@squidproductions.org.
- Remember detected errors and interrupted desktop sessions locally for reporting after restart. Copy or save report text when an email app is unavailable.
- Plan coverage on original PDF pages, add drawing tools and camera movement notes, keep PDF revisions independent, and export lined PDFs.
- Draw coverage before choosing a shot, then create or link a shot with the line’s description, movement, camera, lens, size, and equipment.
- Include script coverage pages and timing notes in the shot list, CSV, and printed reports.
- Preserve line details and Undo/Redo when linking or unlinking shots.

Existing productions, original PDFs, and manual shot notes are preserved.
Reports are reviewed and sent by the user; production files and logs are not attached automatically.
